Silo is a money tracker that works offline. Your records stay on your phone: no account, no sign-up, no ads, and no copy of your data on our servers.

WORKS OFFLINE
Adding expenses, scanning receipts, budgets, reports, split bills and Silo AI all run on your phone, with or without a connection. Silo only goes online for things you can see: exchange rates, the one-time AI model download, and features you turn on yourself (Google Drive backup, your own AI service, bug reports). The Privacy page inside the app lists each one.

SCAN RECEIPTS
• Snap a receipt, import a stack of photos, or share them from any app.
• Silo reads the shop, date, total and items on your phone.
• Receipt Inbox reads a whole stack in the background, then you check and save each one.

TRACK YOUR MONEY
• Cashflow list and calendar with daily totals, search and filters.
• Accounts for cash, bank and e-wallets, with transfers and net worth.
• Reports by category, account or tag.
• Budgets with what's left per day, savings goals and recurring transactions.
• 57 currencies.

SPLIT BILLS
• Split equally, by amount or by item, including your own share.
• Send each person their share on WhatsApp and mark who has paid.
• You can split while adding an expense.

SILO AI, ON YOUR PHONE
• Ask about your spending: "How much did I spend on food this month?"
• Runs on your phone after a one-time model download, so your questions never leave it. Or connect your own AI service if you prefer.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• App Lock with fingerprint, face or phone PIN.
• Hide your totals with one tap.
• Daily automatic backups on the phone, backup files you keep wherever you like, optional backups to your own Google Drive, and CSV export.
• Colour themes and a pure black dark mode.

Silo AI can be wrong, even about your own numbers. Silo is not financial advice. The app is in English.
